# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and scientifically rigorous
community.

Participants must communicate constructively, respect privacy and consent,
welcome differing levels of experience, and distinguish evidence from
opinion. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, publication of private
information, and knowingly misleading scientific claims are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community. Reports should be
sent privately to the maintainers named by the eventual public repository.
Until that contact is established, do not disclose sensitive reports in a
public issue.
